BridgeView

Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

BridgeView shows ships heading for the Blue Water Bridge and can alert you before one goes under it. It doesn't have accounts, ads or analytics, and it doesn't track you.

Your location

If you turn on Only when I'm near the bridge, the iPhone app uses iOS's location services to notice when you arrive within the distance you chose of the bridge, and when you leave. That happens on your phone. Your location is never sent to BridgeView or anyone else; the app only tells our server whether to send you alerts right now.

If you leave the setting off, the app doesn't use your location at all.

What the server keeps for alerts

To send bridge alerts to your iPhone, our server stores:

  • The push notification token Apple gives the app, and the Live Activity token
  • Your alert settings: whether alerts are on, which kinds of ships, minimum length, which directions, and whether Live Activities are on
  • When the device registered and when its settings last changed

These tokens identify the app on your phone to Apple's push service; they don't tell us who you are. Nothing here is linked to your name, Apple ID, email or phone number. If you turn notifications off or delete the app, Apple stops accepting the token and the server deletes the record the next time it tries to alert you.

Server logs

Like most websites, the server sees your IP address when the app or site connects, and keeps it briefly in memory to limit how often one address can make requests. Standard web server logs may also record it for a short time to help keep the service running. We don't use it for anything else.

Ship data and outside links

Ship positions come from AIS, the radio signals ships broadcast publicly, received by a local antenna and the data feeds credited in the app. Ship photos and details come from Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons. Links to VesselFinder, MarineTraffic, Wikipedia and the YouTube ship cam open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your information. Push notifications are delivered through Apple.

Children

BridgeView isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions or requests about your data: email hello@utilityos.com or open an issue on GitHub. See also the terms.
