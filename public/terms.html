BridgeView is a free hobby project that shows ships near the Blue Water Bridge and can alert you before one goes under it. By using the website or the iPhone app you agree to these terms. If you don't, please don't use them.

Not for navigation

Don't use BridgeView for navigation, collision avoidance or anything to do with safety. It's for watching ships, nothing more. Ship positions come from AIS, the radio signals ships broadcast. That data can be late, wrong or missing: not every vessel carries a transponder, some turn theirs off, and our antenna and data feeds miss signals and go down. On the water, use official charts, proper navigation equipment, a lookout and the Coast Guard's notices.

Times and alerts are estimates

Arrival times are worked out from each ship's last reported speed and course, and change when it slows down, speeds up, stops or turns. Alerts are sent on a best-effort basis: they may come early, late or not at all, and a ship may go under the bridge without one. Don't plan anything you can't afford to miss around them.
