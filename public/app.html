BridgeView

See the ships go by

BridgeView lets you know when a ship is about to go under the Blue Water Bridge, so you have time to get down to the river in Port Huron or Sarnia and see it.

What's coming

The ships due at the bridge in the next three hours, with a countdown, how far away each one is, and whether it's heading up to Lake Huron or down to Lake St. Clair.

An alert ten minutes out

Get a notification about ten minutes before a ship reaches the bridge. With Live Activities, the countdown sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island until it's gone under. Pick the kinds of ships, a minimum length and the directions you care about, or only get alerts when you're near the bridge. Your location stays on your phone.

Every ship's story

Photos, where it's headed, what it's probably carrying, when it was built, who runs it, and the last few times it went under the bridge. Got a good shot of a laker? Add it to the ship's page.

Free, no ads, no account

BridgeView is a gift to the Sarnia, Point Edward, and Port Huron communities. Read more about BridgeView, or watch the ships live on the map.
