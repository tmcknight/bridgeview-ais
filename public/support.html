BridgeView

Support

BridgeView lists the ships heading for the Blue Water Bridge between Port Huron and Sarnia, and alerts you about ten minutes before one goes under it, so there's time to get to the river.

I'm not getting alerts

  • Check that Bridge alerts is on in the app's Settings tab.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for BridgeView in the iPhone's Settings app, under Notifications.
  • Your ship filters might leave the ship out: the kinds of ships, the minimum length and the directions in the app's Settings all have to match.
  • With Only when I'm near the bridge on, alerts pause while you're farther away than the distance you chose. The setting needs location access set to Always.

The countdown doesn't show on my Lock Screen

Live Activities need to be allowed for BridgeView in the iPhone's Settings app. Without them you still get a normal notification.

A time looks wrong

Arrival times are estimates from each ship's speed and course, and change as the ship slows down, speeds up or stops. Ships that stop or turn away drop off the list. BridgeView is for watching ships, not for navigation; see the terms.

A ship is missing

Ships show up when their AIS transponder is heard by our antenna or the data feeds we use. Small boats with Class B transponders aren't included in alerts.
